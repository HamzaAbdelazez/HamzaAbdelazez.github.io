About me

From concept to production.

I'm a B.Sc. Software Engineer and current M.Sc. student in Technical Logistics at the University of Duisburg-Essen, with hands-on experience across the whole stack — from technical design all the way to a productive release.

I bring solid knowledge of software architecture, data structures and object-oriented programming, combined with technical curiosity and a quick grasp of new technologies.
